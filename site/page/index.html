01How it works

From a click to your C64

Every Suite app runs on Trom API, and Trom API talks to your cartridge. Whichever app you pick, it all ends up playing on a real Commodore 64.

03Trom Terminal

Your library from the command line

A BBS-style menu for exploring, plus one-line commands your scripts and other tools can call.

  • Guided menu: explore and launch without learning a single command.
  • Stream Player: plays through your library like a streaming service.
  • One-line commands: every action as a command, with --json output that pipes into your scripts.

04Trom Agent

Just ask.

The AI-powered way to drive your TeensyROM and your C64: a plugin for your AI coding agent. Say what you want in plain words, and it plays it, builds the set or sorts out the setup for you.

05Trom API

Drive your cart from your own code

Launch files, search your library, copy files and set themes from a script or a full app of your own. A database of your files and their metadata sits behind it and powers search.

Call it over HTTP from any language, or generate a client from its OpenAPI spec.

  • HTTP
  • OpenAPI
  • Any language

06Get it

What you need, and three steps to play

What you need

Cartridge
A TeensyROM cartridge with current firmware
Commodore
A Commodore 64 or 128
Computer
Windows, Linux, macOS or a Raspberry Pi with a 64-bit OS
Connection
USB or your network

Three steps

  1. Install the suite on your computer.

    On Windows, Linux or a Raspberry Pi, download it:

    Download from the releases page
    Windows x64 · {{windows.size}} Linux x64 · {{linux.size}} Raspberry Pi 64-bit · {{pi.size}}

    On a Mac, use Homebrew:

    brew tap MetalHexx/TeensyROM-Suite https://github.com/MetalHexx/TeensyROM-Suite
brew install teensyrom-suite

    Or let Trom Agent install it and keep it up to date for you.

  2. Connect your TeensyROM over USB or your network.
  3. Play. Run trom ui to open Trom UI, run trom for the guided menu, or just ask Trom Agent.
